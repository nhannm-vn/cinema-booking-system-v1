### 2. LOGIC CHAIN

1. **Step 1 (Model Integrity)**:
   - Observation 1 proves that calling `BookingModel.fromMap(booking.toMap())` throws a null type cast runtime error.
   - Adding `'created_at': createdAt.toIso8601String(),` to `toMap()` matches the `fromMap` contract and eliminates the defect completely.

2. **Step 2 (Data Consistency & Payment Amount Alignment)**:
   - Observation 4 shows `create-vnpay-url` reads `bookings.price_snapshot.total` directly from the database.
   - Observation 2 shows direct client updates on `bookings` are blocked by RLS (`bookings_no_direct_update`).
   - Therefore, when a user selects food (S07) or applies a voucher (S08), the client must re-invoke `hold_seats` with the current `bookingCode`, seats, foods, and recalculated `price_snapshot`. This triggers `on conflict (code) do update`, ensuring the database price snapshot matches the final checkout grand total before payment link generation.

3. **Step 3 (Voucher Calculation Business Rule)**:
   - Observation 3 specifies that voucher discounts apply exclusively to `ticketTotal`.
   - Logic: `ticketDiscount = type == 'percent' ? (ticketTotal * value / 100).round() : min(ticketTotal, value)`.
   - `grandTotal = max(0, ticketTotal + foodTotal - ticketDiscount)`.
   - Food total remains unaffected, fulfilling BUSINESS_RULES.md Section 11 and test PW3 in `TEST_READY.md`.

4. **Step 4 (WebView Payment Interception)**:
   - Observation 4 details the exact redirect URLs from `vnpay-return` (`cinemaapp://payment-success` and `cinemaapp://payment-failed`).
   - Configuring `NavigationDelegate.onNavigationRequest` in `VNPayWebviewScreen` to intercept these custom URI schemes guarantees seamless transition to S09 `ticketSuccess` without requiring native platform deep-link handlers.

5. **Step 5 (Navigation Stack & Back Safety)**:
   - On S09 (Ticket Success), customers must not be allowed to navigate back into the checkout/payment flow.
   - Using `PopScope(canPop: false)` on S09 and routing both "VỀ TRANG CHỦ" and Back press to S03 (`AppRoutes.home`) prevents duplicate payment attempts or invalid state transitions.
